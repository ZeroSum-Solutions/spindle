| D-08 | 2026-10-11 | Until launch, GPT-6.1 Sol (an independent model) reviews every claim and lesson, and receipts are recorded. Phase 3 must add database enforcement that refuses stale or missing reviews for base content (planned ADR 0007, superseding the receipt rules in ADR 0005). A licensed PT reviews the whole base curriculum once before launch. Launch means the first access by real trainers outside test orgs, including pilots. The PT receipt covers exact base versions and hashes, quizzes and selected video segments; that release is frozen, and any later change to a lesson needs a new PT review of that lesson. Supersedes the human-review clause in ADR 0003 and the PT sign-off in the phase 3 row. | Owner decision. The UI never claims PT review before it exists |
| D-09 | 2026-10-11 | Coaching videos are YouTube embeds, never re-hosted: click-to-load privacy-enhanced player, embeddability checked, optional, practice-labelled, never a science source; segments rechecked before launch and every 90 days. | Owner decision; planned ADR 0007 |
| D-10 | 2026-10-11 | Spindle is the evidence-checked base layer. Gyms and coach-creators of any modality build their own method and modality courses on it; phase 4 widens authoring to coach-creators within ADR 0005's ownership boundaries. A private, owner-approved set of coaches inspires style only: their text, images, diagrams and programs are never copied; no paywalled or members-only material is used and no such site is logged into; no coach names, logos or endorsement wording appear in lesson branding; contested frameworks appear only as practice-based context. Videos come from the approved set first; any other channel needs LEAD admission and a GPT-6.1 Sol review. | Owner decision |
| D-11 | 2026-10-11 | A Gemini model (metered, approved lane) may produce a timestamped description of a public, embeddable video segment. It approves nothing; GPT-6.1 Sol reviews the description against SAFETY.md and the lesson. Every admitted clip is spot-checked by a human as part of the D-08 PT launch review, and is marked model-described until then. Spend is capped at $5 per calendar month across all describe calls, and each clip is described at most once per 90 days. | LEAD decision after the 2026-10-11 capability probes (W1); ADR 0007 |
| D-12 | 2026-10-11 | A publisher may record a source-type override for a guideline or position stand that PubMed mislabels, or for an open anatomy or nomenclature reference that supports foundation claims only (plan v3 section 4). The override is stored with its justification and the publisher who recorded it, and is logged. Claims that rely on it still pass Sol review. Applied claims (effects, risks, treatments) still need a systematic review, meta-analysis, position stand or guideline. | LEAD decision after the shoulder claim dry run (W1); ADR 0004 amendment |
| D-13 | 2026-10-11 | Review evidence may go beyond the abstract. For open-licence sources (CC BY, CC0, public domain), the relevant full-text sections from Europe PMC enter the private review packet with a section locator. For a source without an open licence, a publisher may enter a short attributed excerpt (at most 300 words per source, with a page or section locator) into the private packet only. It is never committed to the repository and never shown to learners; it exists for review under fair dealing and fair use for criticism and review. Sol must say when an excerpt is too short to support a claim. | LEAD decision after shoulder dry run 2 (W1); ADR 0004 amendment |
| D-14 | 2026-10-11 | Member-visible tenant free text (brand name, method course title, course blurb, catalog headline and tagline, coach name and role, welcome note) is held until a content publisher records an admission bound to the exact text hash; any edit resets it to pending, and members see fixed fallbacks ("Your gym", "Untitled course") meanwhile. The shared TS/SQL lexical filter is an advisory first screen and can be evaded; the admission is the authority, and reviewers read the whole text. Subtitles and lesson bodies keep the existing practice-based method rules. | LEAD decision after the P4-A2 spec and PR #38 reviews (Sol); docs/curriculum/TENANT-TEXT.md |
| D-15 | 2026-10-11 | Phase 4 brand presence ships without tenant image uploads. Covers are Spindle-drawn patterns in validated literal colours; the header uses a text mark. Logo, coach photo and raster cover uploads move to a later unit (P4-A3) in which every image stays pending and unreadable until admitted, and is served only through a checked route. Anonymous brand delivery moves to P4-A4 (an owner-published frozen projection). Catalog and brand surfaces are member-only. | LEAD decision after Sol spec round 2 found new blocking issues only in the upload surface |
| D-16 | 2026-10-11 | Any brand colour pair a tenant can affect must pass WCAG 2.2 AA in every rendered state, checked identically by the engine and by SQL (unrounded ratios) before a brand is saved. A brand that fails any pair is refused, never adjusted. Selected controls keep the accent, because the validator checks them. | LEAD decision; PRs #34 and #43 |

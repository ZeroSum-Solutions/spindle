# ADR 0007: Review receipts, claim lifecycle, practice media and graded quizzes

- Status: accepted, 2026-10-11
- Supersedes: the receipt and publish rules of [ADR 0005](0005-content-publishing.md) (binding reviews keyed on the current draft, approvals without a ceiling, no lesson review) and its "quiz_attempts are later work" consequence. Everything else in ADR 0005 stands.
- Decisions: D-08 (model review until launch, PT at launch) and D-09 (YouTube embeds), in [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md).

## Decision
- **Receipts are bound to what the reviewer saw.** The trusted recorder builds a packet from `publisher_review_targets` (or `publisher_claim_target`), which reports the database-computed hashes and sorted claim sets. It sends them back with the verdict as `expectedHash` and `claimIds`.
  - `publisher_review_block`, `publisher_review_lesson` and `publisher_review_course` lock the course row against a concurrent save. They then compare the current hash and claim set with the expected ones, and refuse a mismatch as a stale review. A review of content A can never be recorded against content B.
  - `publisher_review_claim` compares the claim hash (statement, evidence level, population and sorted source ids) the same way.
  - A positive verdict (approved claim, supported block, clean lesson, passing segment review) needs a `run`: model, session, packet digest and review time. The run is stored in `review_runs`, apart from the publisher actor recorded in each review's `reviewer` column. A negative verdict may be recorded without a run.
  - Receipts and runs are append-only, server-side only, and written only through the `publisher_*` functions. Clients cannot read them. The service role can read them but cannot write them directly.
- **Lesson and course receipts.** `lesson_reviews` binds a verdict to the exact lesson hash (the lesson as it will appear in the snapshot: practice text and frozen video fields included) and its sorted claim set. `course_reviews` binds the same kind of verdict to the course shell (`private.course_shell`): the snapshot without lesson bodies, meaning course title, subtitle, description, outcomes, part titles and summaries, and lesson order. `clean` means a model run, passing safety and originality checks, and no BLOCK or WARN finding. A table check enforces this for every writer.
- **The publish gate** keeps every ADR 0005 check and adds:
  - a supported binding review must carry a model run;
  - a claim must have a model-receipted approval on its current hash, at most 24 months old, with no source on a video host;
  - a video block must match its registry entry exactly, and the clip must be active, with a passing embed check and a passing segment review in the last 90 days;
  - no image block or lesson media may point at a video host, including rows saved before this rule;
  - **global (founder) content only:** every lesson needs a clean lesson review on its exact hash and claim set, and the course needs a clean course review on its exact shell hash. Gym method courses keep the ADR 0005 path: practice-based, no receipts, and no registry videos.
  - Publishing an unchanged tree returns the latest version. For global content, that only holds if the version follows these rules; a global tree last published under the phase-2 rules gets a new version. Gym method courses stay idempotent whatever rules their latest version used.
  - a course may have at most 1000 graded questions, the most a learner can submit in one attempt;
  - a base lesson may hold claim-free practice quizzes. They are ungraded discussion exercises; only science quizzes are graded. This replaces ADR 0005's rule that every base-layer quiz binds claims.
- **Claim lifecycle.** An approval lasts at most 24 months from both its recording and its model review. The RPC refuses an expiry later than `run.reviewedAt + 24 months` (so re-recording an old run cannot renew a claim), and the gate caps any row at the earlier of `created_at + 24 months` and `review_runs.reviewed_at + 24 months`. An expired or rejected claim blocks new publishes. Published versions are never edited. Instead `version_integrity(version)` tells a signed-in reader each cited claim's state: `current`, `recheck_due` (approval expired), `withdrawn` (rejected after approval; the block loses its Science presentation) or `retracted`. It exposes no receipt detail. Review history is kept.
- **Practice-media registry.** YouTube clips live in `practice_media`, never in `sources`. Video id, segment and rationale are frozen (immutable rows) and copied into a `video` block. The block carries no support field and no claims, so it is always practice-based. Embed checks and segment reviews are append-only (`practice_media_checks`). Status (`active`, `disabled`, `retired`) is append-only and takes effect at once for every version, without a new content version (`version_media`). Provider title, channel and availability sit in `practice_media_metadata`. They are refreshable, served only while under 30 days old, and deleted on retirement. Sources and claims refuse YouTube-family URLs, and so does plain lesson media. A clip plays in a published version only while it is publishable now: status active, and its latest embed check and latest segment review both passed within 90 days. A failed or expired check therefore stops playback at once, like a disable. Every status, check and metadata change locks the clip's row first and rereads its status, so a concurrent activation or refresh cannot undo a retirement. Snapshot parsing (`parseCourse`) stays lenient about video-host media, so phase-2 versions remain readable. New authoring is strict (`validateCatalog` and the database), and the player renders any video-host URL as unavailable, without loading YouTube.
- **Graded quizzes.** A version published under these rules records `rules = 'phase3'` and the sha256 of its graded question set: every quiz block with `support: "science"`, in course order. Question ids are `<lesson id>:q<n>`. `submit_quiz_attempt` takes answers only. The database validates them (every question exactly once, options in range, no other keys) and scores them from the assigned version's snapshot. A trigger rescores any insert, so no caller can store a forged score. A pass needs at least 80% in integer arithmetic. Retries are unlimited, the best attempt counts, and an attempt key makes a double submit count once. `module_status` completes a module when every lesson is complete and a passing attempt exists. That attempt is the learner's own, or one from another version of the same course in the same gym with an identical question-set hash. Versions published before this migration (`rules = 'phase2'`), and courses without graded questions, keep the lessons-only rule. Question ids are positional, because blocks carry no stable id in the snapshot: `<lesson id>:q<n>`, where n counts science quizzes within the lesson. Credit carries only when the whole question-set hash is unchanged, so inserting or reordering a question can never move a pass onto different questions. It only means a new attempt.

- **Model-described segments (D-11, amendment).** A passing segment review is Sol's review of a description, not of the video.
  - A Gemini model (`gemini-3.8-flash`, metered Gemini API) receives the segment natively through Google's YouTube input, with 15 s of context either side. It returns timestamped spoken and visual lines plus everything it could not make out. No download, re-hosting or caption scraping is involved.
  - The describer approves nothing. Sol reviews the description (as fenced, untrusted data), the lessons that embed the clip and SAFETY.md as a `segment` item in the fail-closed harness.
  - `publisher_record_media_check` stores Sol's run as the receipt and the describer run in `describer_model`, `describer_run_id`, `describer_cost_usd` and `described_at`. A passing segment check without a describer run is refused for every writer.
  - A describer failure means not approved. Uncertain content records a failed segment check, which stops playback.
  - Spend and interval are enforced before a call is sent. Under an exclusive lock on the private ledger, the script refuses a call made within 90 days of the clip's last description or call (whatever came of it), or one whose upper-bound cost could take the UTC month past $5. Otherwise it writes a durable reservation at that bound in the current month. The reservation is settled with the cost from the response's usage. An unsettled reservation, or one with an unknown cost (timeout, unreadable response, no usage), keeps its full bound. The bound uses the documented 300 video tokens per second and the Gemini API list prices verified on 2026-10-11, each valid only until a stated date; past it every call is refused (Sol R1 PR20-01, 02, 03, 05, 06).
  - Every description line must carry a valid time inside the described window, and at least one visual line must fall inside the selected segment (PR20-08).
  - Only a `gpt-6.1-sol` run can be recorded as a segment review, and the database refuses any Gemini run as a segment reviewer (PR20-04).
  - A passing segment check stores the lessons it was reviewed with (`context`: lesson id and lesson hash). The database compares that set with the lessons that embed the clip now, under a share lock on their courses. The publish gate requires the clip's latest segment check to cover each lesson exactly as it is. An edited lesson, or a lesson that embeds the clip later, needs a fresh Sol review; `segment-review.ts --reuse <dir>` sends the existing description again with the current lessons (PR20-10).
  - A passing segment check from before this migration (no describer run or lesson context) counts as no segment check (PR20-07).
  - A description counts for 90 days from when it was produced, however recent Sol's review of it. The recorder refuses to record a review of an older description, and eligibility ignores one (R2 PR20-11).
  - Playback in a published version also needs the clip's latest segment check to cover each lesson of that version that embeds it, at the version's frozen lesson hash. A review of an edited lesson does not keep an older version playing (R2 PR20-10).
  - The cost comes from the response's `totalTokenCount`: the prompt at the input price, and the rest at the output price, rounded up. Without a total, the cost is unknown. A call is settled once; an identical retry is accepted, while a conflicting or orphan settlement is refused (R2 PR20-02, PR20-12).
  - `version_media` reports a playable clip as `review: 'model-described'`. No human has checked it until the D-08 launch review, and the player must not claim otherwise.

- **Storage-only lesson media (P18-06).** An image block's `src`, and every lesson media `src`, must be a stored file: `/media/content/...` for any course, or `/media/org/<the course's own gym id>/...` for gym content. Video comes only from registry video blocks: a plain video asset (in lesson media or an image block) is refused at authoring, on every database write (moving a row to another gym included) and at publish; resource links are unaffected.
  - Triggers on `lesson_blocks` and `lessons` refuse anything else for every writer (`private.media_src_ok`).
  - The publish gate refuses rows saved before the rule.
  - `validateCatalog` applies the same rule to authored content (`storedMediaOwner`).
  - Snapshot parsing stays lenient, and the player shows non-storage media as unavailable.

- **Diagram text is reviewed content (Sol R16).** A diagram block is `{kind: "diagram", content: {diagramId, alt, caption, legend: [{key, label, text}]}, support, claimIds}`.
  - All text a learner reads lives in the lesson data and is hashed and reviewed. A science diagram binds claims and needs a binding review, like a paragraph.
  - Code supplies only the drawing geometry keyed by `diagramId` (legend `key`s name its anchors), with no explanatory text.
  - The legacy `{diagramId, caption}` form still parses for old snapshots, but new authoring (`validate_block`, `validateCatalog`) and the publish gate refuse it.

## Why
- Sol's plan review found that a recorder could stamp a verdict onto newer content, and that changed practice guidance could publish with no review (P3-04, P3-05). Both were reproduced on the phase-2 schema before this change. Content-addressed receipts with an atomic compare close both.
- D-08 makes model review the build-time gate for base content. Gyms author their own method content, so the tenant path stays as it was.
- The plan sets 24-month claim validity, 90-day clip rechecks and 30-day provider data. A cap computed in the gate also covers rows written before the rule.
- A separate registry keeps videos out of the evidence chain by construction. Freezing the segment in the lesson hash means a registry change cannot silently alter a reviewed lesson.
- Server scoring from the immutable version means the client never decides "passed" (P3-08). Carrying credit only on an identical question-set hash, within one gym, keeps a pass from surviving changed questions or leaking between gyms (P3-09).

## Consequences
- The database proves which content hash a receipt names, not that a model actually read it. A run row (model, session, packet digest) can back several items, and nothing ties the digest to the targets. The trusted recorder, its private packet log and the digest are the audit trail (phase 3, milestone 2). A dishonest publisher is outside this threat model; a buggy recorder is not, which is what the hash compare catches.
- Every phase-2 fixture now records synthetic receipts through the same RPCs (`model: "synthetic-fixture"`). CI never calls a model and has no approval shortcut.
- Editing a lesson, including only its practice text or its clip, needs a fresh lesson review before the next publish.
- Phase-2 approvals and binding reviews without a run no longer satisfy the gate. They need a receipted review before the content republishes.
- **Answer keys stay server-side (T21; closes the phase-3 risk accepted on Sol S04).**
  - Learners read published versions through `curriculum_versions.learner_snapshot`, a stored generated projection of `snapshot` (`private.redact_snapshot`) in which every graded (science) quiz keeps only its prompt and options. Practice self-checks keep their answers.
  - The `authenticated` role has column-level SELECT on everything except `snapshot`. The raw snapshot stays readable by the service role (publisher tooling) and by SECURITY DEFINER functions, so `submit_quiz_attempt` still scores against the full stored version.
  - Explanations and answers come back through `quiz_attempt_review(attempt)`, which opens only for a passing attempt. With unlimited retries, per-question feedback after a failed attempt would leak the key; before a pass, learners see their score only.
  - `parseLearnerCourse` parses the projection and refuses a graded quiz that still carries its key.
  - Remaining, accepted: the score of each attempt can still be used to infer answers by changing one answer at a time.
- Retraction rechecks before every publish, and the monthly recheck, run in the review harness (phase 3, milestone 2). The database records their results through the existing retraction RPC and claim reviews.

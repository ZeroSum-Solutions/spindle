# Spindle

**Learn the body your clients bring in.**

A muscle spindle is the sensor that tells your body where it is. Spindle does the same for a gym: it teaches trainers anatomy, biomechanics and training science, and puts first the lessons that match what their clients need.

- **Cited science.** Every science claim in the base curriculum is tied to a reviewed source, with its evidence level shown.
- **Your method on top.** Gyms, studios and clinics add their own method (joint-by-joint, fascial lines, sports performance, corrective exercise, Pilates) and assign it to staff. Method content is labelled practice-based and stays private to the gym.
- **Know your clients** (planned). Aggregate client signals, such as how many clients have shoulder restrictions, re-order what trainers learn first, under strict disclosure rules.

> Status: early development. Nothing here is medical advice; Spindle is education for fitness professionals.
